# E-Commerce Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) & Neuro-Psychology Skill

This skill defines the golden standard for CRO, behavioral economics, and frictionless checkout in modern boutique e-commerce.

## 1. Core Principles (Fogg Behavior Model: B = MAP)
- **Motivation:** High perceived value, clear results, skin health guarantees, social validation.
- **Ability:** Remove every unnecessary field. Default to 1-tap interactions, pre-filled inputs, phone masks, and remembered addresses.
- **Prompt:** Contextual triggers at peak moments of intent (e.g. Routine bundle savings, countdown to same-day dispatch).

## 2. Cialdini's Persuasion Levers
1. **Reciprocity:** Free dermatologist routine test, personalized skin guide, free gift with orders over 2000 ₴.
2. **Authority:** Verified dermatological actives (INCI compliance, Cica, Snail Mucin 96%, Niacinamide), official importer certifications.
3. **Scarcity & Urgency:** Same-day dispatch cutoff timer (17:00), limited seasonal bundles.
4. **Social Proof:** Real Ukrainian verified buyer reviews, live recent order notifications, 4.9/5 satisfaction metrics.
5. **Loss Aversion:** Highlighting savings ("Ви заощаджуєте 230 ₴ при купівлі сетом").

## 3. Checkout Friction Elimination
- Never force registration or passwords.
- Always offer Cash on Delivery (Накладений платіж) with 0 prepayment.
- Support Monobank / IBAN instant copy with 1-tap copy buttons.
- Sticky mobile buy bar with safe area inset padding.
